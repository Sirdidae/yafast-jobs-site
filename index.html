About Us

Who We Are

Yafast Job Links & Travel Services sits between the person looking for their next opportunity and everything that stands between them and it — the job listing, the contract, the visa, the flight. We match candidates to genuine, verified openings, then manage every document and travel detail that gets them from home to their first day of work.

What began as informal help for friends and family preparing to travel for work has grown into a full placement and travel service, but the approach has not changed. We walk with every client from their first enquiry to the moment they land — and stay reachable long after that.

We work with candidates across hospitality, domestic and care work, construction, and general labour, connecting them to employers whose contracts we have checked ourselves.

"A placement isn't finished until someone is settled into their new role."
A Message From Our CEO

"When I think about why this company exists, I think about the people who first sat across from me looking for a way out — not out of their country, but out of uncertainty. Someone with a skill and a work ethic, but no way to prove it to an employer thousands of miles away. That is the gap Yafast was built to close."

"What we promise is smaller, and I think more important: that every job we put in front of you is real, that every document we handle is done properly and on time, and that when you walk through the departure gate, someone from our team is standing there with you."

Edward Desire Gondwe
Director / CEO, Yafast

Vision, Mission & Values

🌍

Vision

To be the most trusted bridge between talent at home and opportunity abroad — known as much for how carefully we handle people as for where we send them.

🎯

Mission

To connect job seekers with verified overseas and local opportunities, and to manage every piece of travel and documentation around that move, so no client is left guessing what happens next.

⭐

Core Values

  • Integrity — verified employers and legitimate contracts only.
  • Care — every client is a person first, a file second.
  • Clarity — no hidden steps, no surprise costs, no vague timelines.
  • Follow-through — our job isn't done at the airport gate.
